Use the template below when submitting a manuscript to suggest that the editor considers a CODECHECK for your submission.
Pick the version for your situation: a CODECHECK may already have been completed (e.g., based on a preprint), or you may be proposing one as part of the review process.
Click "Open in email software" to start a new email with the text, or copy it into your cover letter, and replace the placeholders in square brackets.

You are invited to use these templates to write to the authors during a CODECHECK, following the steps of the [codechecker guide](/guide/community-workflow-codechecker).

- Fill in the placeholders: enter the register issue or the certificate ID and click "Fill in from register" to fill in most of them.
- Adapt the text to your check, and delete what does not apply.
- Click "Open in email software" to start a new email with the text, or "Copy text" to paste it into your webmail. The recipients are left empty on purpose. Some email programs cut off very long links; if text is missing, use "Copy text".
- Write to **all authors** in your first email; afterwards, reply to whoever answers and ask them to keep their co-authors in copy.
- Copy the **CODECHECK editor** into your emails, especially if you want them to step in (see [communication](/guide/community-workflow-onboarding#communication)); "Open in email software" adds the editor email as CC.
